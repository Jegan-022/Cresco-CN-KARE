Postal analogy
Network ID = the street name. Everyone on the street shares it.
Host ID = the house number, unique within the street.
Subnet mask = the rule saying where the street name ends and the house number begins.
Default gateway = the post office that forwards letters to other streets.
Broadcast address = shouting to every house on the street at once.
